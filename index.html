Owlet to Huckleberry

Export your baby's sleep history from Owlet, then copy the sleeps you choose into Huckleberry.

Unofficial, use at your own risk. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Owlet or Huckleberry. It uses undocumented APIs, which can break at any time, and automated access goes against both companies' terms of use, which could put your accounts at risk. Logins go straight from this page to the companies' login servers. The only other server involved is the relay (see step 1), which passes along one short-lived Owlet login token and never sees your password or your baby's data.

1Get the sleeps

Your own free Cloudflare relay. One Owlet login step refuses requests from web pages, and the relay forwards just that step. Setup steps are below.
Set up your relay (one time, about 5 minutes, free)

Why it's needed. Owlet's login has three steps. Two of them work from a web page, but the middle one (swapping your Google sign-in for an Owlet token) is set up to refuse requests from web pages. So this page sends just that one request through a tiny relay, which forwards it to Owlet and hands back the answer.

What the relay can see. For that one step it handles a sign-in token that's good for about an hour. It never sees your password, your Huckleberry login, or any sleep data. The relay code stores and logs nothing, and it can only talk to Owlet's login server, nowhere else. (Read the code; it's about 60 lines.)

Why it's yours and not shared. Whoever runs a relay could change its code to keep that token and get into your Owlet account for about an hour. So this page doesn't offer a shared one: you run your own, and nobody else is involved. It's free, and Cloudflare doesn't ask for a card.

  1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com and verify your email.
  2. In the left menu open Workers & Pages (under Compute), then click Create.
  3. Choose Start with Hello World!, name it something like owlet-relay, and click Deploy.
  4. Click Edit code. Delete everything in the editor and paste in the relay code:
  5. Click Deploy (top right).
  6. Copy your worker's address. It looks like https://owlet-relay.yourname.workers.dev. Paste it into Relay URL above, and tick Remember so you only do this once.

To check it's running, open that address in a new tab. It should say "Origin not allowed". That's correct: it only answers this page. To shut it off later, delete the worker in Cloudflare.
