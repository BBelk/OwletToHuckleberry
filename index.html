Owlet Sleep Export

Download your baby's sleep history from an Owlet sock account as a CSV.

Unofficial, use at your own risk. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Owlet. It uses undocumented APIs, which can break at any time, and automated access may go against Owlet's terms of use, which could put your account at risk. Your login goes straight from this page to Google (Owlet's login provider) and Owlet. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else, except the optional relay below.
One Owlet login step refuses requests from web pages. A relay forwards just that step.
